# EPCS extension — kickoff notes

**Attendees:** compliance officer, credentialing office manager, security officer,
two prescribers, pharmacy manager, internal auditor, project lead.

## Scope

- Schedule II-V controlled substances through the existing e-prescribing module.
- Individual practitioners credentialed through the hospital credentialing office.
- Pharmacy application changes are out of scope; the pharmacy receives through the
  existing intermediary.

## Key dates

- The third-party EPCS audit is booked. It cannot move, and nothing may be prescribed
  electronically for controlled substances before the audit has passed.

## Discussion

Prescribers said the current paper process for Schedule II takes several minutes per
prescription and that the new process must be quicker than paper, or they will keep
using paper.

The security officer and one prescriber disagreed about biometrics. The security
officer prefers fingerprint plus token; the prescriber refused to use a fingerprint
reader. Both options satisfy the regulation if two distinct factors are used.

The internal auditor reminded everyone that last year's audit found shared passwords
on the ward (see the internal audit finding).

## Actions

1. Credentialing office to document the identity-proofing procedure.
2. Security officer to confirm token model and validation level with the vendor.
3. Compliance officer to prepare the access-control approval procedure.
