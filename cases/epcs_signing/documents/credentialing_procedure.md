# Credentialing office procedure CR-12 — EPCS identity proofing (draft)

1. The practitioner attends in person with a government-issued photographic ID.
2. The credentialing officer confirms that the photo matches the person presenting.
3. The officer confirms that the state licence to practise and, where applicable, the
   state authorisation to prescribe controlled substances are current.
4. The officer confirms that the DEA registration is current and in good standing.
5. The officer records the identity-proofing event in the credentialing register.
6. Credential activation information is issued through two separate channels: the
   token is handed over in person and the activation code is sent by post.
7. Where a practitioner holds several DEA registrations, each one is linked to the
   practitioner's account.

A separate team in IT enters the signing permissions into the application after the
list of approved practitioners has been approved by two people.
