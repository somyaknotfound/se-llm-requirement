# Internal audit finding IA-2025-07 — shared credentials on ward 4B

**Auditor:** Olivia Grant
**Practitioner interviewed:** Dr. Karim Haddad, DEA BH4285715, NPI 1245319599

## Finding

During the unannounced ward audit, one workstation was logged in under a single
prescriber account for an entire shift and used by three clinicians. A password was
written on a note attached to the monitor.

## Risk

For controlled substances this would allow a prescription to be signed by someone
other than the practitioner whose DEA number appears on it.

## Recommendations

1. The signing function must only be usable by the practitioner whose DEA number is
   on the prescription.
2. Signing permissions must be reviewed whenever a practitioner leaves the practice,
   and revoked on the day of departure.
3. The application's audit trail must record every signing event with the identity
   of the person who signed.
