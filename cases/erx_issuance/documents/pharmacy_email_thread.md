**Subject:** Cancelled prescriptions and missing information

Hi all,

Following up on the kickoff. Three problems we see every week with the current
process:

1. Cancellations. We find out about cancelled prescriptions by phone, sometimes
   after we have already dispensed. We need the cancellation electronically and we
   need to be able to respond so the prescriber knows it landed.

2. Missing refill counts. About one prescription in twenty arrives without the
   number of refills, and we have to call the clinic.

3. Substitution. We often cannot tell whether the prescriber allows a generic, so we
   hold the prescription and call.

Could the new module refuse to send a prescription that is missing the refill count
or the substitution indication?
