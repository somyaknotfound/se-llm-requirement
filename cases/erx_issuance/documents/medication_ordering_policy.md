# Medication ordering policy (excerpt) — MOP-7, revision 4

## 3. Who may order

3.1 Medication orders may be entered only by licensed prescribers with active
ordering privileges. Students and trainees may draft orders, which a supervising
prescriber must review and sign.

3.2 Verbal orders are not permitted for outpatient prescriptions.

## 4. High-alert medications

4.1 Anticoagulants, insulin, opioids and oral chemotherapy are high-alert
medications.

4.2 A high-alert outpatient prescription must be verified by a second licensed
clinician before it is transmitted to a pharmacy.

## 5. Alerts and overrides

5.1 Prescribers must review every interaction and allergy alert presented at the
time of ordering.

5.2 Overriding an alert requires a documented reason.

5.3 Duplicate therapy — two active orders for drugs in the same therapeutic class —
must be flagged to the prescriber before signing.

## 6. Patient information

6.1 The patient's weight must be recorded within the last 30 days before a
weight-based dose is prescribed for a patient under 18.

6.2 Prescriptions must identify the patient by full name and date of birth.
