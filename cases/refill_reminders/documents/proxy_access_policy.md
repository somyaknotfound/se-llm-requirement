# Patient portal proxy access policy (excerpt) — PP-3

1. An adult patient may grant proxy access to another adult by recording consent in
   the portal or app.
2. The consent record must state who the proxy is, what they may do, and when consent
   was given.
3. The patient may revoke proxy access at any time; revocation takes effect
   immediately.
4. A proxy acts under their own login and never with the patient's credentials.
5. Every action a proxy takes is recorded against the proxy's identity.
6. Proxy access for patients aged 13-17 requires review by the health information
   management team.
