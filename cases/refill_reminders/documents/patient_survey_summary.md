# Patient survey — medication refills (summary, 412 responses)

- 58% have run out of a long-term medication at least once in the past year.
- The most common reason (71%) was forgetting to request a renewal in time.
- 64% would like a reminder; the most requested lead time was one week.
- 39% have a family member who helps manage their medications.
- 22% said small text in the current app is a problem.
- Free-text comments asked for a way to see whether a renewal request had been
  received and whether the pharmacy had the medication ready.
