# Software Requirements Specification — Electronic Prescribing of Controlled Substances (EPCS)

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose

This specification states the requirements for electronic prescribing of controlled substances (epcs) as elicited from 5 stakeholder roles and 5 project documents, grounded in the authorised knowledge base.

### 1.2 Scope

Extension of the hospital network's e-prescribing module to Schedule II-V controlled substances: identity proofing and credential issuance for practitioners, logical access control over the signing function managed by two individuals, two-factor authentication at signing, digital signature and archiving of the signed record, transmission to the pharmacy, an internal audit trail with daily analysis and incident reporting, monthly prescription logs, and a third-party audit before the application may be used.

- S-CRED-09
- S-PRES-04
- S-PRES-06
- The system shall not allow the transmission of an electronic prescription if an original prescription was printed prior to attempted transmission.
- The system shall not alter the contents of the prescription during transmission.
- The system shall notify individuals designated under § 1311.125 or § 1311.130 within one business day of discovering that the hard token has been lost, stolen, or compromised.
- The system shall remove signing permission within 15 minutes of receiving a revocation event.
- The system shall support fingerprint readers on managed workstations.
- The system shall support the network identity provider (OpenID Connect) for workforce sign-in.
- The system shall support the vendor OTP hard token as a second factor.
- The system shall use mutual TLS calls between the application and the identity provider.
