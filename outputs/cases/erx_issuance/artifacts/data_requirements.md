# Data requirements

| Requirement | Statement | Acceptance criteria | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| FR-ERX-002 | The system shall provide a patient's active medication list and recorded allergies on the same screen as the order being written. | The patient's active medication list and recorded allergies must be visible on the same screen as the order being written. | S-PRES-03 |
| NFR-ERX-008 | The system shall retain records for the required period. | Records are retained for the required period as specified by regulatory requirements. | S-COMP-02; S-COMP-05 |
| FR-ERX-018 | The system shall enable prescribers to complete a routine prescription for non-controlled medications in under 90 seconds through the EHR interface. | Prescribers can create, transmit, and receive electronic prescriptions for non-controlled medications through the EHR interface within 90 seconds. | DOC-KICKOFF#p2; S-PO-09; S-PO-14 |
| FR-ERX-019 | The system shall process the cancellation of electronic prescriptions. | The prescriber can initiate the cancellation of an issued electronic prescription, and the pharmacy receives a notification of the cancellation. | DOC-KICKOFF#p2; S-PO-10; S-PO-15 |
