# Software Requirements Specification — Electronic Prescription (e-Rx) Issuance

_Generated by the documentation agent. Every requirement below is pending human approval; see the approval queue for its status._

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose

This specification states the requirements for electronic prescription (e-rx) issuance as elicited from 7 stakeholder roles and 5 project documents, grounded in the authorised knowledge base.

### 1.2 Scope

Issuance of an electronic prescription by an authorised prescriber in a hospital network's EHR, including real-time drug-drug interaction and allergy contraindication checking at the point of ordering, formulary checking, and transmission, change and cancellation of the prescription with community pharmacies through the e-prescribing intermediary. Controlled substances are handled by a separate project.

Out of scope:

- After go-live, change control permits quarterly releases.
- Clinical subject-matter experts are available at a monthly clinical safety board and fortnightly design reviews.
- The EHR exchanges orders over legacy HL7 v2 interfaces and pharmacy traffic goes through an existing e-prescribing intermediary.
- The module must be ONC-certified before a go-live date fixed by the network's regulatory calendar.
- The project is run by a four-hospital network with 18 engineers across two sites.
- The project shall not be delayed due to regulatory calendar constraints.
- The system shall not support the issuance of controlled substances.
